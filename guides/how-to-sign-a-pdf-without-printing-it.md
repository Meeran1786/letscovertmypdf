To sign a PDF without printing it, open [Sign PDF](/sign-pdf), choose your file, create your signature by drawing, typing or uploading it, then drag it into place and download. It takes about a minute, and you skip the printer and the scanner entirely.

Sign PDF runs in your browser, so the contract or form you are signing is never uploaded. That matters for documents with your address, salary or ID number on them.

## Steps

1. Open [Sign PDF](/sign-pdf) and choose your PDF.
2. Create your signature. **Draw** it with your mouse, finger or stylus, **Type** your name in a handwriting style, or **Upload** a photo of your signature on paper.
3. Find the right page. The preview opens on the last page, because that's where most signatures go. Use the arrows to move between pages.
4. Drag the signature to the signature line. Pull the blue dot in its corner to make it bigger or smaller, or use the arrow keys to nudge it.
5. Tick **Add today's date** if the document asks for a date next to your signature.
6. Tap the blue **Sign PDF** button, then download the signed file.

> Tip: tick **Remember my signature on this device** and it will be waiting under a **Saved** tab next time. It is stored only in your own browser.

## Draw, type or upload: which should I use?

Upload gives the most natural result if you already have a signature on paper. Sign a blank white sheet with a dark pen, take a photo, and upload it. The white background is removed automatically, so only the ink sits on the page.

Draw works well on a phone or tablet, where you can sign with your finger. On a laptop, a mouse signature can look shaky, so Type or Upload is often neater.

Type is the quickest. It writes your name in a handwriting-style font, which is fine for many everyday forms.

## Signing every page

Some documents, like leases or loan papers, ask for initials or a signature on every page. Tick **Same spot on every page** and your signature is added in the same position on each page in one go.

If the pages need signatures in different places, sign the file once per page instead, each time using the signed file from the step before.

## Is a signature added this way legally valid?

It's a visual electronic signature: an image of your signature placed on the document. Many everyday documents accept this, such as rental forms, school permission slips, invoices and offer letters.

Some contracts require a certified digital signature, which uses a certificate to prove who signed and that nothing changed afterwards. This tool doesn't add that kind of signature. If you're unsure, ask the person who sent you the document what they accept.

## Questions

### Can I sign a PDF on my phone?

Yes. It works in Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android. Drawing with your finger is often easier on a phone than with a mouse.

### Is my signature saved anywhere?

Only if you tick **Remember my signature on this device**, and then only in your own browser. You can delete it any time with **Forget saved signature**. We never receive it.

### The PDF has form fields too. Can I fill them in first?

Yes. Use [Fill PDF Form](/fill-pdf-form) to type your answers, download the filled form, then open that file in Sign PDF to add your signature.

### Is it free?

Yes. There is no watermark and no signup needed. Every tool is unlimited, and it keeps working offline once the page has loaded.
